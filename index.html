    Before you call

    Most answers are here. For anything else, WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us.

    Do you have a scooty?

    Yes, the Honda Activa 6G. Choose your dates at the top of the page and book it, and we'll send you the price on WhatsApp.

    Can you tell me the price on the phone?

    The price depends on the bike and how long you keep it, so we need your pickup and return date and time first. Send them through the booking form and we'll reply on WhatsApp with an exact quote.

    How long can I rent for?

    You can rent for 12 or 24 hours, counted from your pickup time. Longer trips run in 24-hour days from pickup. We'll confirm the exact rent for your dates when we reply.
